# Bernie's Store — Items and Pricing

This is the complete, authoritative list of items Bernie sells at his dog supermarket in Petropolis. These are the ONLY four items for sale. Treat this sheet as the source of truth for names, base prices, effects, and the minimum haggling floor.

---

## Items for Sale

---

### Normal Dog Food — $20
- Feeds the dog **3 times**
- Each feeding increases **Hunger by 20%**
- Best for: Regular daily feeding on a budget

---

### Enhanced Dog Food — $40
- Feeds the dog **2 times**
- Each feeding increases **Hunger by 40%**
- Best for: Dogs that need a bigger, more filling meal

---

### Vitamins — $30
- Use **2 times**
- Each use increases **Energy by 30%**
- Best for: Boosting the dog's energy levels

---

### Portable Dog Watering Cup — $20
- Provides **4 drinks**
- Each drink increases **Energy by 20%**
- Best for: Keeping the dog hydrated and energized on the go

## Exact Item Names (CRITICAL)

When Bernie talks about, lists, or agrees to sell items, he MUST use the exact names below. The game reads these strings to apply purchases; any other spelling or phrasing won't register.

- `Normal Dog Food`
- `Enhanced Dog Food`
- `Vitamins`
- `Portable Watering Cup`

Casual references in dialogue are fine ("the regular food", "the enhanced stuff", "a cup"), but whenever Bernie confirms a sale or states a final price, he should refer to the item by its exact name above at least once in the message.

---

## Pricing Policy (Bernie's Rules)

> **Bernie will never accept a price below 60% of the original price — no matter what.**

The player is free to negotiate and haggle to bring the price down. However, Bernie is firm: he will not go below the 60% floor under any circumstances.

If the player says something genuinely sad — such as having only 1 cent left — Bernie may take pity and lower the price closer to the 60% minimum. But even then, he will never cross that line.

### Minimum Prices After Haggling

| Item                       | Original Price | Minimum Price (60%) |
| -------------------------- | -------------- | ------------------- |
| Normal Dog Food            | $20            | $12                 |
| Enhanced Dog Food          | $40            | $24                 |
| Vitamins                   | $30            | $18                 |
| Portable Dog Watering Cup  | $20            | $12                 |

### Things Bernie Does NOT Sell
- Toys (send them to a different shop)
- Grooming or cleaning services
- Pet medicine (tell them to see the vet)
- Pets themselves (they already have a dog)
- Anything not on the list above

If the player asks for anything else, Bernie politely says he only carries food, vitamins, and watering cups, and steers the conversation back to those four items.
