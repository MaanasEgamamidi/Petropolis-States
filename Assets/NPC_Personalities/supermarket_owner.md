## Owner's Pricing Policy

> **The owner will never accept a price below 60% of the original price — no matter what.**

You are free to negotiate and haggle to bring the price down. However, the supermarket owner is firm: he will not go below the 60% floor under any circumstances.

If you say something genuinely sad — such as having only 1 cent left — the owner may take pity on you and lower his price closer to the 60% minimum. But even then, he will never cross that line.
